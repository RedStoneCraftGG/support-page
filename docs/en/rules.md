# Server Rules

This page explains the rules of the Venity Network server that all players must follow. These rules are established to maintain comfort, security, and fairness within the server.

## 1. No Use of Cheats or Any Kind of Client Modifications

The use of cheats, hacks, or client modifications that give unfair advantages (such as x-ray, hitbox, fly hacks, ESP, and other cheating modules) is strictly prohibited. If you are caught using cheats, you will be banned immediately and must wait for the ban duration to expire.

If you believe you were banned without reason, please contact Venity Network staff or admins on [Discord](https://venitymc.com/discord)

## 2. No Bug Exploitation

Jika menemukan bug atau kesalahan di server, laporkan kepada staff. Eksploitasi bug untuk keuntungan pribadi akan dikenai sanksi.

## 3. No Spamming or Using Inappropriate Language

Do not send repeated messages in a short time or use offensive and inappropriate language in the server chat (in any language).

::: info
If the violator is in a guild chat, it is the responsibility of the guild officers and the guild leader.
:::

## 4. Respect Other Players and Staff

Always be kind and respectful to other players and the server's staff team. Bullying, harassment, or derogatory behavior **will not be tolerated**.

## 5. No Promotion of Illegal Client Modifications or Cheats

Players are prohibited from promoting or suggesting the use of illegal client modifications or cheats not allowed by the server. Any modification that provides an unfair advantage is strictly prohibited.

## 6. Use Your Own Account

Do not share your account with other players or use someone else’s account. Accounts used by more than one person may be subject to penalties.

## 7. Do Not Create Disturbances in the Server Community

Players are prohibited from creating disturbances or chaos within the server community, whether through server chat, forums, Discord, or other communication platforms linked to the server. This includes provoking, spreading false information, creating conflicts between players, or spamming. Such behavior disrupts the comfort of other players and may lead to strict action from the staff team.

## 8. Do Not Share Personal Information

To maintain security, do not share personal information (such as addresses, phone numbers, etc.) of yourself or others on the server.

## 9. Obligation to Read and Follow Additional Rules

The server may have additional or temporary rules for specific events or situations. Be sure to always follow the latest updates and abide by these rules.

## 10. Repeated Violations Will Be Strictly Enforced

Players who repeatedly violate the rules or commit serious offenses may face a permanent ban from the server.
